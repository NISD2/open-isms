# NIS2/BSIG Sanctions & Fines (Section 65 BSIG)

Source: [OpenKRITIS](https://www.openkritis.de/it-sicherheitsgesetz/nis2-umsetzung-gesetz-cybersicherheit.html)

## Market Context

Germany estimates ~30,000 affected companies, of which only 17% have taken fundamentally sufficient measures ("Grundsatz ausreichende Massnahmen"). The remaining 83% (~24,900 companies) need to achieve compliance.

## Management Liability (Section 38 BSIG)

- Management bodies **must** implement cybersecurity risk management measures and monitor their implementation (Section 38(1))
- Violation triggers **personal liability** of management towards the institution (e.g., Section 93 AktG for stock corporations)
- The institution **may** make claims for compensation; a waiver or grossly disproportionate settlement is **void** (Section 38(2))
- Management must **regularly attend training** to ensure sufficient knowledge and skills to assess risks and take appropriate action (Section 38(3))
- Heads of federal government institutions are exempt from management classification

## Key Takeaways for Platform Users

1. **Documentation is legally required** (Section 30(1) sentence 3) -- not documenting compliance is itself a fineable offense (up to EUR 10M for essential entities)
2. **Management is personally liable** -- not a corporate shield, personal liability under Section 38
3. **Proportionality applies** -- measures must be "appropriate, proportionate, and effective" (Section 30(1) sentence 2), meaning SMEs can implement lighter measures if they document the reasoning
4. **Reporting timelines are strict** -- 24h early warning, 72h notification, 1-month final report. Missing these is a EUR 10M/7M offense
5. **BSI can order audits at any time** for essential entities (proactive supervision under Section 61)
6. **Important entities face reactive supervision** (Section 62) -- BSI acts only on evidence of non-compliance, but all measures must still be implemented
