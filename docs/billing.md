# Billing (`lib/billing/`)

Rules learned from three review passes over the first billing PR, and from the ones since. Each one prevented a real defect. `CLAUDE.md` keeps the index; read this before touching anything under `lib/billing/`, an export route or a sign-off procedure.

- **One order service, `placeOrder` in `lib/billing/place-order.ts`.** The order page (tRPC `billing.place`) calls it, the admin close must call it too when it is built, and nothing else creates an invoice. The order page only orders at a price the customer was shown (`quotedGrossCents`). The price comes from the server, never from a browser.
- **One function decides the price: `holderNetCents` (`lib/billing/holder-price.ts`).** It prices the account holder, not the account level: a grandfathered person (`isGrandfatheredPerson` in `lib/billing/access.ts`: stamped `grandfatheredAt`) pays 2.400 net on every order, renewal and re-order, even while their account is full; everyone else pays 4.800. `billing.status`, `billing.quote`, `placeOrder` and `closeNetCents` all call it. Never price from `accessLevel`.
- **One cancel service, `cancelSubscription` in `lib/billing/cancel.ts`**, behind `billing.cancel` (`payerProcedure`, the account holder only). Inside the thirty days (`cancelWindow` in `lib/billing/money-back-window.ts`, asked through `cancelWindowFor` in `lib/billing/money-back.ts`: the order day is day 0, day 30 is the last, counted in Berlin, and a recorded waiver ends them early) it issues a full credit note in Qonto, which cancels the invoice paid or not, writes the `credit_note` row and sets the account to `unpaidAccessLevel` (grandfathered for a grandfathered holder, else free). `refund_owed` is Qonto's live status read just before the credit note: `paid` means owed; any status other than paid or unpaid refuses and alerts. After day 30 it only sets `renewal_canceled_at`; access runs to `period_end`. The credit note follows the order's safety pattern exactly: number from `document_number_counter` series `credit_note` (prefix `GS`, committed before the call, sandbox never touches it), the account lock, an `order_check` mark with the GS number, `alertOperators` on an unclear answer. The confirmation is `billing.canceled`, with the credit note PDF from `lib/billing/deliver-credit-note.ts`, archived to `billing/<account>/<GS number>.pdf`. **A cancel builds its confirmation as a `CancelNotice` (`lib/billing/cancel-notice.ts`) before anything is sent:** `cancelSubscription` sends it at once; `cancelForErasure` (self-deletion) hands it back, and the erasure confirmation carries it with the credit note attached (`encloseCancelNotice`), so the person gets one letter. The accounting address on the Qonto client still gets the credit note on its own. If the erasure fails after the cancel, the notice goes out on its own, worded for an account that stays.
- **An unpaid invoice suspends the licence by itself (AGB B9), and its payment reopens it.** `lib/billing/non-payment.ts` decides, `non-payment-schedule.ts` runs it every hour inside the app (deployed server, ordering on, advisory lock). The day after the due date printed on the invoice (Berlin), a full account whose latest uncredited invoice Qonto reports `unpaid` gets `revokeAccess`, the same fallback as the manual revoke; an account below full whose latest uncredited invoice Qonto reports `paid` within its licence year goes back to full, but only when its latest access change in the audit log is a B9 suspension: a revoke by an operator (`billing.access_revoked`) stays until a person undoes it. A Qonto answer that cannot be read changes nothing, so an outage never locks out a paying customer. Each change and its audit row (`billing.access_suspended`, `billing.access_reopened`, with the level before and after) are written in one transaction, so neither stands without the other; then the operators are mailed. B9 promises the customer no notice. The billing and offer pages reuse a readable Qonto answer for five minutes; the hourly run always asks live.
- **Every invoice tells the operators** (`internal.new_sale`, `lib/billing/sale-notice.ts`), sent from `placeOrder` once the invoice is recorded, to `PLATFORM_ADMIN_EMAILS`. It is good news, so it stays apart from `alertOperators`, whose mail always asks for work. Sandbox orders say so in the subject.
- **Refunds are manual and live in the Subscriptions tab** (`lib/billing/subscriptions.ts`). A credit note moves no money and our key cannot send a transfer, so a refund owed stays on the tab until a platform admin marks it done (`refund_done_at` and `_by`, audited). A credit note on a paid invoice alerts the operators with the amount and the last day (`refundDueDay`: the cancel day plus thirty, as the AGB promise), and marking it done emails the holder `billing.refund_sent` (`lib/billing/refund-sent.ts`); a holder who deleted their account gets nothing, and the toast says so. **"Unpaid" in Qonto is not proof that no money came:** customers pay by transfer, Qonto flips to paid only once it matches one, and a credited invoice never flips afterwards. So every credit note on an unpaid invoice alerts the operators and sits under "Watch for a late payment" for ninety Berlin calendar days (derived from `refund_owed = false` and `created_at`, no flag); "Payment arrived, refund owed" sets `refund_owed` when a transfer turns up. Customer copy never tells an unpaid canceler there is nothing more to do. Revoke sets a full account to `unpaidAccessLevel` by hand, audited. Door two ("New customer") lives on that tab too.
- **Who may order is `lib/billing/ordering.ts` plus the account holder.** Off while Qonto or the invoice prefix is unusable, platform admins only against the sandbox, everyone live; and only `billing_account.ownerUserId` (`payerProcedure`). Never a company role: any member may add an organization and is its admin. `billingFor` (`lib/billing/ordering-access.ts`) decides whether billing exists for a visitor: `/bestellen`, `/billing` and the sidebar entry. Platform admins get it whenever Qonto is configured; everyone else needs live keys. **Only nisd2.eu sells** (`isSellerInstance`, judged by the configured address, never by the keys): a new account starts free there (`newAccountAccessLevel`), and grandfathered anywhere else (a self-hosted instance, a sandbox, a local run), because nobody could order. A broken key on nisd2.eu closes ordering for a while and grandfathers nobody.
- **The pricing launch ran once on nisd2.eu, and its code is gone.** It stamped `user.grandfatheredAt` on everyone who had got in, moved their free accounts to grandfathered and froze them into a newsletter group, which the Pricing tab still sends the announcement to (`lib/billing/announcement-group.ts`). The `billing` and `walkthrough` feature flags are retired (`lib/feature-flags.ts`); their enum values stay. **Grandfathering belongs to the person:** an account opened later by a stamped person starts grandfathered too (`newAccountAccessLevel`), and the promo link stamps one more person (`lib/billing/promo-grant.ts`).
- **The access gate is on by default** (`server/trpc/init.ts`): `companyProcedure`, `adminProcedure` and `reviewerProcedure` refuse an effective free account. `accountProcedure` / `accountAdminProcedure` are the exemptions by tier, used by billing, notifications, `assessment.updateCompany` and the supplier portal. The one exemption by name is `FREE_WALK_PROCEDURES` (`lib/billing/access.ts`): the calls the walk's free steps make (`isFreeStep`), each of which checks the step itself; renaming a listed procedure closes it to free accounts. A new router is gated unless it deliberately uses an account tier. The portal layout sends a free account to `/bestellen` except for `FREE_PORTAL_PATHS` and the walk's home and free steps (`WALK_HOME_PATHS`, `WALK_FREE_PATHS` in `lib/walkthrough.ts`).
- **Exports and sign-offs follow one state machine** (`lib/billing/account-state.ts`, facts from `account-facts.ts`): free, grandfathered, trial, licensed; allow or confirm. Anyone may export and sign off, an account without an order included; whether a page is reachable is the paywall's business. Only inside an order's money back (trial) does the first one ask first. Every export route (`exportAccess`, the policy PDF, the full printout) and every sign-off (`signOffProcedure`, `review.approve`, `durchgang.approvePolicies`) asks it; the page asks first with `useBillingAct`. A confirm records the waiver on the order's first invoice (`billing.waiveMoneyBack`), which ends the thirty days (AGB B7). Orders under terms before `PAY_ON_USE_SINCE` keep their unconditional money back (`paysOnUse`).
- **Nothing on our side may fail after Qonto has issued an invoice or a credit note.** Everything that can refuse (the account lock, an unexpired uncredited invoice, a price that moved since the quote, which Qonto client this account may use) is decided before `createInvoice`. A Qonto client belongs to one billing account, so a client another account holds is never reused. When Qonto's answer is unclear (timeout, 5xx, or the order breaking part way), an invoice may exist: the customer is told not to retry, `alertOperators` mails the platform admins, and an `order_check` row blocks every further order for that account on the server until a platform admin clears it in the Pricing tab (`lib/billing/order-check.ts`). The row is committed on its own just before the Qonto call and deleted inside the order's transaction once Qonto answered clearly, so it exists whenever an invoice might, and never for a failure before the call. The order therefore locks its account `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`: `FOR UPDATE` would make that insert, whose foreign key points at the locked row, wait on the order itself. Every case a person must act on goes through that alert, never only a log line.
- **We deliver the invoice, not Qonto** (`lib/billing/deliver-invoice.ts`): poll for the PDF, archive it to S3, and send it from our address as `billing.invoice`, with Qonto's public link as the fallback. It runs after the order commits and can never undo it.
- **Every email is a React Email component** in `lib/mail/emails/` (one file per email, default export with `PreviewProps`), drawn in the shared frame and parts from `lib/mail/components/`. The builders in `lib/mail/templates.ts` keep their names, write the subject and the plain-text twin, and render the HTML with `renderEmail` (`lib/mail/render.ts`), so every builder is async and every caller awaits it. Paragraphs and headings are plain elements with the exact inline styles; React Email's `Text` and `Heading` would change sizes and margins. Preview and check client compatibility with `bun run email:dev` (React Email's preview server, its Compatibility tab).
- **Billing and erasure mail is a letter about one document** (`documentEmail`, drawn by `lib/mail/emails/document-letter.tsx`): a heading, the greeting, a card with the document's name, number and the facts someone acts on (amount, due date, refund date), then the managing director's signature. The words live with the document (`order.ts`, `cancel-terms.ts`, `lib/gdpr/confirmation-mail.ts`). **The sending address and `SUPPORT_EMAIL` take no mail**, so a letter invites replies only through `letterReplyTo` (on nisd2.eu the published `SELLER.email`, `contact@nisd2.eu`; elsewhere none). Mail in a person's voice (welcome, lifecycle nudge, course follow-up, the newsletter unless `NEWSLETTER_REPLY_TO` is set) replies to `replyAddress()`: `contact@nisd2.eu` on nisd2.eu, the instance's `SUPPORT_EMAIL` elsewhere.
- **Every email names who sends it, and only optional mail offers an opt-out.** `emailLayout` takes the reader's language for every message; optional mail passes its `PreferenceFooter`, essential mail only `{ locale }`. On nisd2.eu (`isSellerInstance`: the configured `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL`, exact hostname, never lib/env's default) the footer carries the § 35a GmbHG details from `lib/billing/seller.ts`; a self-hosted install gets the neutral line instead. The frame is tables with a `width="560"` attribute, not divs, because Outlook on Windows renders with Word and ignores `max-width` on divs.
- **Door two is `closeDeal`** (`lib/billing/close-deal.ts`, platform admin Subscriptions tab): finds or creates the customer's user (no password, a draft company and its own account through `createDraftCompany`), then calls the same `placeOrder` with `source: "admin"` and an optional agreed net (`netCentsOverride`, admin only; door one always passes null). A customer who has never signed in gets an `account.setup` link: a seven-day, single-use token in `email_otp` (purpose `account_setup`, sha256 of the secret, `lib/auth/setup-link.ts`), opening `/auth/setup` to set a password or continue with Google. Both doors render the same fields (`components/billing/OrderFields.tsx`) and quote through `lib/billing/quote.ts`.
- **Tax follows the VAT number, never the address.** The country in the VAT number decides German VAT, reverse charge or none. The order service prices once and invoices that price.
- **VIES informs, it never blocks.** An outage or an invalid answer is recorded, never an error. Our own VAT number is the requester, parsed by the same `splitVatNumber` as a customer's; one that cannot be read is sent as no requester, never as a wrong one.
- **Credentials go only to Qonto's two hosts, over https.** The host picks the pair (sandbox pair and staging token for the sandbox, production pair otherwise), with no fallback between them. Anything else yields no config.
- **Settings live in `lib/billing/config-schema.ts`**, included by `lib/env.ts`. Billing modules take settings as parameters and never read `process.env`. A wrong billing value switches billing off; it never stops the app booting.
- **Qonto is the source of truth for invoices.** Our tables keep only what cannot change after issue, including the VAT amount, the VAT treatment and the VIES consultation number, plus one fact of ours written once: the money-back waiver. CHECK constraints keep a row from contradicting itself. Status is always read from Qonto.
- **Invoice dates are calendar days in `Europe/Berlin`.** Real invoice numbers must come from `document_number_counter` by upsert, committed before Qonto is called, so they are unique even when a call fails. The sandbox number is for the sandbox only.
- **Tests use synthetic identifiers.** Generate VAT numbers and IBANs that pass their checksum; never a real company's or our own account's.
- **Migrations run when the container starts** (`scripts/runtime-migrate.mjs`), so merging a migration is the moment it runs on production. The previous release keeps serving until the new container is healthy, so a migration must work with the previous release's code: add in one release, and set NOT NULL, drop or rename in the next.
