## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out what Kardashev Catalyst UG personnel may and may not do with company assets, systems, and data. It applies to all employees and founders.

---

## 2. Devices

**Company-issued devices (MacBook Pros):**
- FileVault full-disk encryption must remain enabled at all times
- Screen lock activates after ≤2 minutes inactivity (MDM-enforced: do not change this)
- Automatic OS and software updates must not be indefinitely deferred. Security updates: apply within 48 hours of release
- 1Password must be installed and used for all credentials: no credentials stored in browsers or plaintext files
- No software installed from untrusted sources (outside App Store, Homebrew, or official vendor binaries)

**Personal devices:**
- Personal devices must not be used to access production systems, customer data, or internal credentials unless specifically authorised and the device meets the same encryption and MDM requirements as company devices
- Exception: Signal on personal phones is acceptable for incident coordination

---

## 3. Credentials and accounts

- Every person has their own account for every system: no shared accounts
- Passwords are generated by 1Password (≥16 characters) and never reused
- MFA is mandatory on all accounts: see Access Control Policy (NISD2-ISMS-007) for required methods
- Credentials are never shared verbally, via email, or via Slack/Teams. Use 1Password sharing for any temporary credential handover
- If you suspect a credential has been compromised: change it immediately and notify the CISO

---

## 4. Data handling

**Confidential data** (customer compliance records, evidence files, personal data, credentials, source code):
- Stored only in approved systems (Google Drive, GitHub, S3, PostgreSQL)
- Transmitted only via approved encrypted channels (TLS, Signal, Google Drive sharing)
- Never stored unencrypted on local device storage
- Never sent via unencrypted email
- Never shared with unauthorised parties

**Labelling:** Files containing confidential data should be named with a `CONFIDENTIAL_` prefix where practical.

**Production data:** Production customer data is never used in development, testing, or demonstrations. All development uses synthetic seed data.

---

## 5. Physical security

- Laptops are never left unattended in public spaces without screen lock active
- Laptops are never left in unattended vehicles
- When working in co-working spaces or cafes: use a screen privacy filter for sensitive work
- Lock screen whenever stepping away from the device, regardless of expected absence duration
- Report lost or stolen devices to the CISO immediately (within 1 hour of discovery): remote wipe will be initiated

---

## 6. Remote working

Kardashev Catalyst UG operates as remote-first. When working remotely:
- Do not use public/untrusted Wi-Fi for accessing production systems or customer data without additional protection (hotspot from phone, or verify network security)
- Co-working spaces are treated as untrusted networks: MFA protects all accounts regardless
- Home networks: ensure router firmware is up to date and uses WPA2/WPA3

---

## 7. Software and services

Do not sign up for new SaaS tools that will process customer data, personal data, or confidential company information without approval from the CISO. This triggers a supplier security review and potentially a DPA requirement.

Approved tools list: maintained in the supplier register (nisd2.eu/suppliers).

---

## 8. Incident reporting

Report any of the following to the CISO immediately via Signal:
- Lost or stolen device
- Suspected credential compromise
- Suspicious email, login alert, or unexpected access notification
- Any security event you're uncertain about: report first, investigate together

There is no penalty for reporting a false alarm. The cost of not reporting is potentially severe.
