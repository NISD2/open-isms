# Lesson 4.1: Final Assessment

You have completed the CRA SBOM Fundamentals course. This final lesson is the [[attestation quiz]].

The course covers approximately 60 minutes of reading across 9 lessons.

The quiz has eight questions drawn from all four modules. You need to answer at least six correctly (75 percent) to pass. You may retake it as many times as you need.

When you pass, the platform generates a dated PDF [[attestation]]. Keep this PDF with your [[technical documentation]] records. A [[market surveillance authority]] reviewing your CRA compliance may ask to see evidence that your team has been trained on SBOM obligations.

## What you have covered

**Foundation** — What an SBOM is and why CRA Article 13(5) made it mandatory. The connection between the SBOM and the Article 14 vulnerability reporting obligation. The timeline: CRA in force December 2024, reporting obligations September 2026, full compliance December 2027.

**Module 1: What goes into an SBOM** — The core fields every component entry needs: name, version, supplier, [[PURL]], and hash. Why exact version strings matter for vulnerability scanning. The difference between [[top-level dependency|top-level]] and [[transitive dependency|transitive]] dependencies, and why the CRA minimum (top-level) differs from best practice (full transitive). The 10-year retention rule under Article 31 and who can request access.

**Module 2: Formats** — [[CycloneDX]] 1.6: the OWASP standard designed for vulnerability management, with native [[VEX]] support and strong tooling integration. [[SPDX]] 2.3: the ISO/IEC 5962:2021 standard designed for license compliance, with GitHub native export and multi-format output. Both qualify for CRA Article 13(5). Choose CycloneDX if Article 14 vulnerability reporting is your primary driver.

**Module 3: Building and maintaining** — Source-time tools (cdxgen) for application dependency graphs. Image scanners ([[Syft]]) for OS and runtime packages. CI/CD integration so every release gets an SBOM at build time. Vulnerability monitoring with [[Grype]] and the CISA KEV catalogue. OWASP Dependency-Track for multi-product continuous monitoring. The Article 14 escalation decision tree.

Take the quiz below when you are ready.
